Voice & Tone

Cairn strives to look, feel, and function consistently

The same goes for how we speak

Principles

Three habits behind every sentence we write.

Plainspoken

Short words, short sentences. If a ten-year-old could not follow it, rewrite it.

Warm

We talk like a colleague who is glad you asked, never like a help-desk script.

Curious

A little wit is welcome. A joke at the customer's expense never is.

Try it

Pick a surface, then ask for another way to say it.

In the wild

Say it like you would across a table.

Say this

“Your files are safe. We checked twice.”

“Something went wrong on our side. Try again in a minute.”

“Invite a teammate”

Not this

“Data persistence has been verified across all redundant nodes.”

“Error 5032: The operation could not be completed.”

“Initiate collaborator provisioning”
