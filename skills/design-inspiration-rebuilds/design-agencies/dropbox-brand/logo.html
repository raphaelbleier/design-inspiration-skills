System

The Cairn logo has two expressions.

A stack of three stones, balanced, never perfect. Use it wherever people need to know who we are.

Full lockup

The mark and the wordmark, side by side.

Whenever there is room, the lockup comes first. The wordmark is always set in lowercase weight, never stretched.

Tab

When space shrinks, the tab steps in.

The blue tab holds the mark alone. It is built for app icons, favicons and the corner of a page.

Clear space

Give the logo room to breathe.

Keep a margin equal to the height of one stone, x, on every side. Nothing enters this zone.

Minimum size

Small is fine. Illegible is not.

The lockup never drops below 96px wide on screen, the tab never below 16px.

Colour

Three colourways, no improvising.

Blue on white, white on blue, deep ink on cyan. Anything else goes through the brand team.
