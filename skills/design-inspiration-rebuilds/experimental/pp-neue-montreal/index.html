From Vieux-Port
to Petit-Marais

Port-Marais is an invented harbour city where northern energy meets old-world charm. Built on a tidal island, it is known for its rich history, diverse culture and restless creative spirit. The city blends old and new effortlessly, from cobbled quays to a glass skyline. Many languages can be heard in its neighbourhoods.
