      Questions

      Good to know

      What makes Brio different?

      One idea: a clean can. Low sugar, plant-based caffeine, real fruit and a loud fizz.

      Is it carbonated?

      Yes. We chose a strong, fine bubble so the flavour stays sharp to the last sip.

      How much sugar and caffeine?

      4 g of sugar and 32 mg of caffeine per 100 ml, 17 kcal.

      Any artificial colours?

      None. The colour comes from fruit and vegetable extracts only.

      Who should skip it?

      Children, pregnant women and anyone sensitive to caffeine.

      How should I store a can?

      Cool and out of direct sunlight, and best served very cold.
