Open the landscape

for the quiet that gathers
in the long grass of your hours.
(from “Salt Before Morning”)

There is a door in the weather
that opens only inward,
and a kettle left warm for no one.

Stay with the small light.
It has walked a great way
to find the shape of your window.
(from “The Late Lamp”)
