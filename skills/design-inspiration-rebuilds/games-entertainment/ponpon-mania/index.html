behindthe story

Mochi Parade is a loud, soft-hearted marmot with a borrowed pair of sunglasses and a plan to become the neighbourhood DJ. Chaos in the kitchen, big dreams on the roof, and a lot of late-night noise.

two friends, one very loud marmot

Mochi Parade started as a late-night joke between two friends who could not agree on what a comic should do when it is read on a screen. One drew, one wrote code, and neither wanted to give up the other half.

Every chapter is a record you can flip through: swipe the covers, press play, and the pages answer back. We keep the drawings rough on purpose, because rough drawings feel like somebody made them.

thanks for reading. see you at the next chapter.
