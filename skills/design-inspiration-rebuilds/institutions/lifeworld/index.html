Glasshour is a multi-site artwork made by Mara Hollin, presented in Bergen, Leeds, Boston and Taipei, and online.

Glasshour is a layered artwork created by artist Mara Hollin.

Commissioned by Northfield in collaboration with Courier, Glasshour is site-specific, created especially for harbour squares and crossings.

Glasshour is an invitation to consider who you are and where you are, here and now.

We often treat the places we cross every day as if they were corridors. In a time defined by speed and multitasking, our attention is spread across many windows at once.

This steady stream of signals carries on while a file uploads or a message sends. It is an efficient space for sharing things with friends, colleagues and family.

In these moments of heightened activity, the artwork loosens your sense of depth and time, and shows the immediate place anew.

On display at 20:24, screens that usually show crisp, loud advertising dissolve into the soft, slow, liminal perspectives of Glasshour.
