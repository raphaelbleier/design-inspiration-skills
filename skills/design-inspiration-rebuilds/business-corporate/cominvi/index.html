S.02OUR SERVICES

Leveraging deep geological insight and engineering prowess, we partner with clients to turn raw opportunity into operational excellence.

Engineering

We design and plan safe, efficient underground infrastructure tailored to each site's geology.

Ground Support & Fortification

We stabilise workings with anchors, mesh, shotcrete and steel frames to secure fractured ground.

Development

We execute precise tunnel development for ramps and access points by drilling, blasting and excavation.

Production

We extract mineralised material with controlled methods tuned for productivity and dilution control.

Hauling

We move blasted ore and waste with scooptrams, low-profile trucks and efficient logistics.

    S.05TECHNOLOGY

    Our underground fleet features more than 360 specialised machines. Every unit is selected for durability, safety and adaptability in confined, high-stakes environments.

    • Motor Grader

      Precision machines that level and shape roads, ramps and working platforms.

    • Telehandler

      Material-handling units with extendable booms for restricted spaces.

    • Compactor

      Vibratory machines that consolidate loose material for ramps and platforms.

    • Face-Drilling Rig

      Electrohydraulic rigs with rock-drill booms for accurate horizontal drilling.

    • Load-Haul-Dump Loader

      Low-profile loaders for loading, hauling and dumping in confined headings.
