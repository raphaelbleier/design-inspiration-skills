Boundless Horizons

Along one of the most game-rich bends of the Umzi River, the Kopano Reach Collection brings together two lodges and three camps. For nearly a century this land has been guarded, and it now enters a new chapter of patient stewardship.

We stand for balance, where people and wildlife thrive together. Rooted in partnership with the neighbouring villages, our ethos is simple: when our people prosper, so does the wilderness.

Accommodation

Each lodge and camp is its own expression of the bush, from earthy nostalgia to quiet contemporary comfort. Wildlife drifts through the riverine thicket day and night, so there is no need to leave your deck to begin.

Lodges

Two lodges offer privacy, generous proportions and unhurried interiors. Every suite opens straight onto the river, where water draws the widest variety of animals and birds. Plunge pools, indoor and outdoor showers and long living rooms keep you close to the bush.
