About us

Val Oren is a new place of calm in the northern Alps, where old shepherd craft meets contemporary design. Every beam, every stone and every terrace is shaped to let the valley in, and to give guests and owners a home that grows more valuable with time.

Concept

The architecture of the complex grows out of the mountain, keeping its strength in stone and its warmth in wood. Panoramic terraces open onto views where the spirit of the valley lingers, and tradition intertwines with modernity to create a new format of recreation: deep, meaningful, and filled with the energy of nature and local craft.

Where The Mountains Speak

Val Oren sits at the foothills of the Nordkar range, in the very heart of Hallmere, the old capital of alpine craft and clear air.
